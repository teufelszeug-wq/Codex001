## Next

### M7 Mystery / SF Verification
Evidence/alibi/knowledge-state verification and science/assumption checks.

M7 will use the same author-controlled pattern as M5/M6 but adds explicit ledgers:

Mystery:
- Evidence Ledger
- Testimony / contradiction graph
- Alibi and event-time constraints
- Character Knowledge State
- clue / reveal timing and fairness metadata

SF:
- Assumption Ledger
- hardness level
- units and order-of-magnitude checks
- communication delay / causality
- relativity / time-dilation constraints where enabled
- scientific terminology and assumption-boundary diagnostics

Deterministic violations must remain distinct from advisory or model-assisted findings.

### M8 AI Co-Author
Bible-aware generation constrained by Canon, dependency freshness and Lint.

The AI Co-Author must not silently consume stale, unresolved or DISPUTED dependencies as confirmed facts. Generated/inferred material remains outside CANON until author promotion.

### M9 Closed Beta
External long-form creators and measurement.

Measure:
- false-positive rate by pack/rule
- finding triage behavior
- Earth-Origin Guard precision
- M6 social-rule usefulness
- structured-ledger adoption
- dependency fan-out
- graph size
- revision retention needs
- selective-revalidation savings

Revision retention/compaction, graph partitioning and large catalog expansion should be driven by these measurements rather than guessed prematurely.

## Planning rule

Every design meeting may add a sub-milestone when work belongs to the current phase, or a top-level milestone only when it creates a distinct product capability or dependency boundary.

Sub-milestones use Mx-Sn notation when a decimal-numbered top-level milestone already exists, avoiding ambiguous identifiers.

Ideas move through IDEA → PROPOSED → ACCEPTED → SCHEDULED rather than disappearing from the plan.
